Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Thế Chín TikTok content analytics application accesses, uses, and protects information obtained through TikTok APIs.

1. Information We Access

The application may access basic TikTok account information and public video-related data that the user explicitly authorizes through TikTok's authentication and authorization process.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include basic profile information, public video information, video descriptions, publishing dates, and performance metrics such as views, likes, comments, and shares.

2. How Information Is Used

The information is used only for personal content management, performance analysis, research, and improving content strategy.

The application does not use TikTok data for unauthorized advertising, profiling, spam, or unrelated purposes.

3. Data Sharing

TikTok user data is not sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect any data processed by the application. Access to TikTok data is limited to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
